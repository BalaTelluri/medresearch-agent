Genomics

From variant to evidence.

Exact allele matching, cited literature and a transparent ML test sample.

Research only. Not a diagnosis, clinical validation, or a therapy recommendation. Use public or demo variants only. Variant coordinates are sent to gnomAD; missing ClinVar matches are queried from the public ClinVar snapshot. Gene searches go to PubMed. Do not upload real patient data.

Variant input

GRCh38 only. Paste CHROM-POS-REF-ALT, or load a VCF (max 20 alleles, 200 KB). No liftover. Indels must already be left-normalized.

ClinVar: 28 Sep 2026 snapshot. gnomAD: v4. Cached frequencies show retrieval time. Genotypes are not analyzed.

What the model sees

Sequence lengths, base composition, transition type and molecular consequences. No ClinVar labels, gene identity, review status, disease names, or record IDs are used as features.

The classifier is a baseline, not an ACMG classification system. Its score is not a clinical probability.

Held-out-gene test sample

Evaluation limits and confusion matrix
Balanced sampling changes prevalence. Known ClinVar variants and annotations differ from novel patient variants. No external clinical cohort or prospective testing. Gene holdout limits one leakage path, not all biological dependence.
Evidence will appear here
Each allele keeps ClinVar evidence separate from population frequencies and the experimental prediction. Missing data and failed sources are shown, not filled in.
